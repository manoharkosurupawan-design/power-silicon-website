Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with Power Silicon Technologies — from IP security protocols and EDA toolchain licensing to engagement models and delivery timelines.

Power Silicon Technologies operates with a **90% core focus on Digital VLSI Engineering** (Micro-architecture, Verilog/SystemVerilog RTL design, UVM verification, Physical Design, STA timing closure, and GDSII signoff) and a **10% complementary focus on Embedded Systems & Firmware Support** (Linux BSP, RTOS drivers, and post-silicon board bring-up).

We execute comprehensive mutual non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) before reviewing any technical data. All engineering work is performed within isolated Virtual Private Clouds (VPCs) with zero-leakage security policies, two-factor authenticated VPNs, and strict physical and network segregation. Customer retains 100% of all generated IP, RTL code, testbenches, and GDSII databases upon milestone completion.

We operate across all Tier-1 industry EDA toolchains:
• Synopsys: Design Compiler, ICC2/Fusion Compiler, PrimeTime, VCS, SpyGlass.
• Cadence: Genus, Innovus, Tempus, Xcelium, JasperGold.
• Siemens EDA: Calibre DRC/LVS/PEX, Questa.
Our tape-out experience spans planar 180nm/65nm down to FinFET 28nm, 16nm/12nm, 7nm, and 5nm nodes across TSMC, Samsung, Intel IFS, and GlobalFoundries.

We provide three flexible engagement models:
1. Turnkey SOW: Fixed-cost milestone delivery from architectural spec to GDSII tape-out.
2. Offshore Dedicated Center (ODC) / Staff Augmentation: Senior engineers working directly as dedicated extensions of your internal design team.
3. Time-Critical SOW: Rapid task-force deployment for immediate bottlenecks (e.g. timing closure crunch or verification signoff before tape-out deadlines).

Upon mutual NDA execution and technical requirement review, we typically provide preliminary project scoping and resourcing plans within 48 to 72 hours. Dedicated engineering teams can be mobilized and integrated into your Git and compute cluster within 1 to 2 weeks.
