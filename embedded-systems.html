10% Complementary Practice

Embedded Systems, Firmware & BSP

Bridging the gap between silicon tape-out and commercial deployment with low-level software bring-up, Board Support Packages (BSP), and RTOS drivers.

Software Meets Silicon

Enabling Your Custom Silicon with Production Firmware

While our core 90% engineering focus is on digital VLSI hardware, custom silicon requires robust software to function in the real world. Our dedicated 10% Embedded Systems practice exists precisely to bring custom ASIC/SoC silicon alive.

From developing U-Boot bootloaders and Linux kernel device drivers to porting FreeRTOS and Zephyr onto custom RISC-V and ARM cores, our firmware engineers ensure your software teams have working software stacks on Day One of silicon arrival.

Embedded & Firmware Capabilities

  • Linux Kernel & Yocto BSP: Board bring-up, Device Tree creation, and custom kernel patch development.
  • RTOS Porting: FreeRTOS, Zephyr, and ThreadX ports for microcontrollers and edge AI SoCs.
  • High-Speed Peripheral Drivers: PCIe Gen4, USB 3.2, MIPI CSI/DSI, Gigabit Ethernet, and I2C/SPI/UART drivers.
  • Secure Boot & Cryptography: Hardware Root of Trust, Secure Bootloaders, and ARM TrustZone integration.
