Proven Methodology

The Power Silicon Execution Pipeline

Our structured silicon engineering lifecycle ensures predictable execution, first-pass tape-out success, and flawless embedded enablement.

STAGE 01

Ideation & Architecture

System specification, PPA budgeting (Power, Performance, Area), and IP selection.

STAGE 02

RTL & Micro-Arch

Modular Verilog/SystemVerilog design, bus interconnects, and UPF low-power intent.

STAGE 03

UVM Verification

Constrained-random testbenches, assertions, coverage metrics, and formal verification.

STAGE 04

Physical Design & Signoff

Hierarchical floorplanning, placement, clock tree synthesis (CTS), and routing.

STAGE 05

Signoff & Tape-Out

Multi-corner STA, DRC/LVS verification, IR drop analysis, and GDSII foundry release.

STAGE 06

Embedded Bring-up

Post-silicon validation, BSP driver development, and hardware-software integration.
