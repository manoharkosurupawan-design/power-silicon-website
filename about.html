Company Profile & Vision

Architecting the Silicon of Tomorrow

Rooted in the philosophy of "Design • Innovate • Deliver", Power Silicon Technologies Pvt. Ltd. is an engineering-first semiconductor house dedicated to VLSI excellence and embedded integration.

Who We Are

Semiconductor Engineering with Rigor & Precision

Power Silicon Technologies was established to bridge the growing engineering gap in next-generation microelectronics. Modern computing demands ultra-low power envelopes, exceptional compute density, and zero silicon re-spins.

With **90% of our core resources focused on Digital VLSI Design, Advanced Verification, and Physical Signoff**, we partner with global fabless semiconductor companies, Tier-1 automotive OEMs, and hyper-scale innovators to tape out complex SoCs and ASICs on time.

Our dedicated **10% Embedded Systems practice** ensures that your silicon comes alive immediately with production-grade Board Support Packages (BSPs), device drivers, and post-silicon validation routines.

Core Principles

The Cornerstones of Our Practice

Every project at Power Silicon Technologies is governed by four immutable engineering commitments.

01

Technical Rigor

No shortcuts. We enforce exhaustive linting, CDC/RDC checks, and 100% formal and functional coverage closure before signoff.

02

Zero Re-Spin Policy

Tape-out respins cost millions. Our multi-corner STA signoff and silicon validation checklists guarantee first-pass functionality.

03

Strict IP Isolation

Complete client data segregation with military-grade network security, encrypted design servers, and robust legal NDA frameworks.

04

Flexible Delivery

From full turnkey design ownership to targeted ODC staff augmentation, we adapt seamlessly to your engineering pipeline.
