Privacy Policy, Terms & IP Protection

Our commitment to uncompromising client intellectual property security, strict data protection, and transparent engineering engagement standards.

1. Intellectual Property & Strict NDA Commitment

At Power Silicon Technologies Pvt. Ltd., we recognize that semiconductor design files, RTL source code, testbenches, PDK technology files, and tape-out GDSII databases represent the core intellectual property and competitive advantage of our clients.

• Full Ownership: Unless explicitly agreed otherwise under a proprietary IP licensing model, all work products, custom RTL, test plans, synthesis scripts, timing constraint files (SDC), and physical layouts developed under client Statement of Work (SOW) agreements remain the exclusive property of the client.
• Strict Segregation: All engineering workspaces are partitioned across isolated virtual environments with end-to-end data encryption and restricted access controls.
• Standard NDA Execution: We execute bilateral or unilateral Non-Disclosure Agreements prior to receiving any proprietary customer documentation or technical specifications.

2. Privacy Policy & Data Collection

This Privacy Policy outlines how Power Silicon Technologies collects, uses, and safeguards information submitted through our official website:

• Information Collected: When you submit an engineering RFQ inquiry, job application, or newsletter subscription, we collect your name, business email, phone number, company name, and technical project scope.
• Use of Information: Contact information is utilized strictly to provide engineering quotations, schedule technical briefings, or evaluate employment applications. We do not sell, rent, or distribute your personal or corporate data to third-party advertisers.
• Cookies & Analytics: Our site uses minimal, privacy-respecting session cookies to ensure seamless navigation and responsive layout rendering.

3. Terms of Service & Engagement

All commercial projects undertaken by Power Silicon Technologies Pvt. Ltd. are governed by signed Master Services Agreements (MSA) and formal Statements of Work (SOW).

• Milestone Acceptance: Turnkey deliverables are verified against pre-agreed acceptance criteria (e.g. 100% clean DRC/LVS, zero timing setup/hold violations at signoff corners, 100% functional coverage).
• Warranty & Post-Silicon Support: We provide structured engineering support through tape-out GDSII release and assist through initial silicon bring-up.
• Jurisdiction: Commercial contracts are subject to the laws and jurisdiction specified in the executed MSA.
