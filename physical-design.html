Physical Design & Tape-Out Signoff

Flawless Netlist-to-GDSII backend execution: hierarchical floorplanning, low-skew CTS, multi-corner STA timing closure, and DRC/LVS foundry signoff down to 5nm FinFET.

Backend Mastery

Closing Timing & Physical Signoff on Advanced Process Nodes

Shrinking process geometries introduce severe physical design challenges: on-chip variation (OCV), electromigration (EM), IR drop, signal integrity (crosstalk delay and noise), and complex design rule checks (DRC).

Power Silicon Technologies possesses extensive tape-out experience in leading foundry nodes from TSMC, Samsung, GlobalFoundries, and Intel. We utilize Synopsys ICC2/Fusion Compiler and Cadence Innovus to deliver optimal Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) with zero design rule violations.

Backend Signoff Checklist

  • STA Multi-Corner Closure: 0 setup/hold violations across all PVT (Process-Voltage-Temperature) operational corners.
  • Physical Verification (DRC/LVS/Antenna): 100% clean Siemens Calibre / Synopsys ICV signoff.
  • Power Integrity (IR Drop & EM): Static & dynamic IR drop analysis within ±2.5% VDD thresholds via RedHawk / Voltus.
  • Foundry-Ready GDSII / OASIS Delivery: Final mask stream verification with dummy metal fill insertion.

Floorplanning & Power Grid

Hierarchical partitioning, macro placement optimization, IO pin assignment, and low-impedance power mesh design (VDD/VSS).

  • Macro channel routing & blockage allocation
  • Dedicated power straps & via matrix design
  • Power switch cell insertion for UPF domains

CTS & Clock Distribution

Advanced Clock Tree Synthesis (CTS) utilizing multi-source CTS, mesh trees, and clock concurrent optimization (CCOpt) for minimum skew.

  • Sub-50ps clock skew targets on GHz cores
  • Low-power clock gating & buffer sizing
  • Useful skew optimization for setup/hold balance

Routing & STA Closure

Crosstalk-aware detailed routing, timing ECO generation, SI (Signal Integrity) glitch prevention, and final multi-mode multi-corner (MMMC) closure.

  • PrimeTime / Tempus distributed signoff
  • Automated physical ECO & metal fill
  • GDSII / OASIS streaming & tapeout release
