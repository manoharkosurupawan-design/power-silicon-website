Exhaustive DV Methodology for Mission-Critical Silicon

More than 70% of modern ASIC/SoC project schedules are consumed by design verification. Power Silicon Technologies deploys seasoned DV architects equipped with advanced coverage-driven verification (CDV) frameworks to catch the most elusive corner-case bugs.

We build scalable, modular UVM environments containing reusable Verification IP (VIP), scoreboard reference models in C++/SystemC, and automated regression tracking pipelines.

Verification Signoff Criteria

  • 100% Functional Coverage: Defined covergroups matching 100% of the verification plan items.
  • 100% Code Coverage Closure: Line, toggle, branch, condition, and FSM coverage with full waiver justification.
  • Formal Property Proofs: Mathematical proof for critical arbitration and security logic.
  • Clean Gate-Level Simulation (GLS): Zero-delay and SDF-annotated timing simulations with dynamic power profiling.

UVM / SystemVerilog Testbenches

Complete testbench creation with UVM agents, sequences, drivers, monitors, and scoreboards with randomized stimulus.

  • Custom UVM VIPs (AXI, PCIe, USB, UART)
  • C/C++ DPI-C reference models
  • Automated nightly regression suites

Formal Verification (SVA)

Exhaustive mathematical property proofs using JasperGold and VC Formal to prove unreachability of bug states.

  • SVA assertion development & binding
  • Formal deadlock & livelock proofs
  • Sequential Equivalence Checking (SEC)

Gate-Level Sim & Power Signoff

SDF-annotated gate-level simulations across fast, typical, and slow process corners to validate clock domains and power intent.

  • Multi-corner SDF timing back-annotation
  • VCD/FSDB generation for dynamic power
  • UPF Low Power Sim with power switching
